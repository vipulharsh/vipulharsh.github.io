Topology design for small-scale datacenters (DCs)

Small-scale datacenters are far more numerous than large-scale DCs yet almost all research has gone into designing better topologies for hyper-scale DCs. We discovered that topology design is a fundamentally different problem at small-scale with different factors at play. Specifically, the asymptotic characteristics as nodes → ∞ do not hold true at small scale-- there are small-scale topologies that outperform existing leaf-spine DCs across diverse workloads despite their bisection bandwidth being O(n) worse asymptotically!
